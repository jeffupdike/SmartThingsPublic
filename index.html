The plan

How we take your home from listed to sold.

Every presentation we build follows this path, tailored to your home, your timeline and your neighborhood.

Step 01

Price it right

A market analysis built from current NTREIS data, with the comparable sales shown so you can see exactly how we got to the number.

Step 02

Prepare it well

A room-by-room plan for repairs, staging and timing that puts your money where buyers will notice it, and nowhere they won't.

Step 03

Launch it everywhere

Professional photography and video, a polished listing, and a coordinated debut across every channel we have.

Step 04

Negotiate

Honest feedback after showings, a clear read on every offer, and two negotiators in your corner instead of one.

Step 05

Close

We track every contract date and deadline from the option period through closing, so nothing slips and nothing surprises you.
